---
title: "A notebook idea for the travellers"
date: "Mar 24 2026"
published: true
---

How cool would it be if you kept a notebook while travelling, and for each stranger you meet and get to know, they write down the story of their life on one of those pages? Or it can be their life advice, a drawing, whatever. You gave them the freedom to create. And by the end of that trip, you have a notebook full of real human stories. 

So when you're unmotivated, when you're struggling, you can look at those pages and see again the beauty of this world?
